Goal Bingo

Privacy

Goal Bingo stores only what the game needs. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What we store

  • Each bingo group: its name, card size and reveal date.
  • Each player: the name they typed, and a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of a random key kept in their browser, so the same browser can edit its own card. We never store the key itself.
  • The squares on each card, who wrote them, and whether they are ticked.
  • For rate limiting only: a hashed, shortened form of the visitor’s IP address with a counter, deleted within an hour.

Anyone with a group’s link can see that group’s cards. Friend squares are hidden from everyone except their writer until the reveal date. Group pages are not listed in search engines.

In your browser

Your browser keeps your key and a list of your groups in local storage. Nothing else, and no cookies.

How long

A group’s creator can delete it at any time, and any player can leave (which removes their card and the squares they wrote). Groups nobody has opened for 500 days are deleted automatically.

Who processes it

The site runs on Cloudflare (hosting and database). We do not share or sell data to anyone.
