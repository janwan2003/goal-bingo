Questions

What is a goal bingo card?

A goal bingo card puts your goals for the year on a bingo grid instead of a list of resolutions. Each time you do one, you cross it off; a full row, column or diagonal is a bingo. A 5 × 5 card has a free square in the middle.

What do my friends write?

Each friend writes one square on your card: a dare, a wish, something they know you keep putting off. You cannot see them until the reveal. When you finish a square a friend wrote, that friend confirms it.

Do my friends need an app or an account?

No. They open the link, type a name and they are in. It works in any phone browser, including inside WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram.

Can I use it for things other than New Year?

Yes: a summer bingo, a trip bingo, a uni term or a birthday year. Pick any reveal date, or reveal it yourself at a party.

Is it free?

Yes, completely. There are no ads and we do not sell anything.

Can I share my card?

Yes. Save your card as an image sized for stories, with your friends’ squares highlighted.
