# goal-bingo

## Thesis

**Who:** Friend groups, sisters and cousins, couples-of-friends and work teams who make New Year
"bingo cards" of goals instead of resolutions. The trend has run on TikTok, Lemon8 and in lifestyle
press since the 2022/23 New Year, and groups already make the cards together at craft nights.

**What:** A no-login group bingo of the year where your friends write part of your card. One person
starts "Our 2027 bingo" and sends the link. Each member fills most of their own 5×5 (or 3×3) card,
and every other member must add one "wildcard" square to each friend's card ("Finally go to the
dentist", "Visit me in Lisbon", "Learn to make a proper roast"). Wildcards stay hidden until
1 January, when every card reveals with its friend-written squares. Through the year, members tick
squares; ticking a friend-written square needs that friend to confirm ("witness"). The group board
shows everyone's card, lines completed and who got bingo first. Monthly nudges ("3 squares this
month?") and a 31 December "year wrapped" reveal close the loop.

**Needs others:** yes for the core: the wildcard squares and witness ticks come only from friends,
and the race for first bingo needs a group. (A solo card is possible but is exactly what free
template makers already do, so the product only offers group cards.)

**Viral moment:** 1 January reveal: "My friends put these on my 2027 card" is a natural story post,
and the trend already has people posting their bingo cards. Second moment: "BINGO" cards when a
line completes, posted to the group.

**How often:** start-of-year ritual (the next one is in 6 weeks), monthly check-ins, plus shorter
cards: "Summer bingo", "Trip bingo", "Birthday year bingo", "Uni term bingo". Frequency is the
weak point; priority 3.

**Why now:** New Year is 41 days away; the bingo-card trend is still current for 2026 (GMA, AOL,
Bustle, Kapwing guides) and groups already "compete to see who gets bingo first" (GMA). Existing
apps are install-only, and the web tools are solo template makers (Bingo Baker, Canva, Kapwing).

### The six questions

1. **Why the group IS the input:** part of every card is written by the other members, and ticks on
   those squares need their confirmation. Without others the card has blank wildcard squares and
   no race.
2. **Reveal moment:** midnight 1 January: wildcard squares flip open on every card at once. Later
   reveals: each "BINGO" and the 31 December year-end card.
3. **Ritual or deadline:** cards lock at midnight on New Year's Eve; monthly check-in day; year-end
   reveal. Other cards (summer, trip) get their own deadlines.
4. **30-second join:** open link → nickname → write one wildcard square for each friend (a few
   words each) → submit. Filling your own card can come later; the join itself is under a minute.
5. **Screenshot-worthy result:** your card with friend-written squares highlighted and their names
   ("from Ana"), sized for stories; the group board showing who is closest to bingo.
6. **Why a joiner creates their own:** the trend is personal, so many members will want a second
   card with family or a work team (a work team doing this went viral via Upworthy); the reveal
   page ends on "Start a bingo with another group" plus summer/trip templates.

## Our edge

- **Loop: needs others** (wildcard squares, witness ticks and the bingo race all come from friends).
- **K estimate ≈ 1.3** = 6 invited per group × 70% who join (writing one funny square for a friend
  is fun and fast) × 30% who start a card with another group (family, work team). Reason: the trend
  is personal and seasonal, so people want one card per circle; but it is a once-a-year start, which
  caps the third factor. Weakest factor: joiner → creator outside January.
- Beats install-only apps on join friction and beats template makers on the group mechanics; the
  friend-written wildcard is the new angle nobody offers.
- **Risks:** seasonality (the window is roughly 20 Dec – 15 Jan), trend fatigue (punch cards),
  long-term retention through the year. Wildcards are word-filtered and the card owner can swap one
  wildcard they dislike.

- Joiner → creator: "Start a bingo with another group" with New Year / Summer / Trip / Uni term links that prefill.
