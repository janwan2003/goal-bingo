Goal Bingo

Your bingo card, written with friends

Goal Bingo is a free group goal bingo card. Everyone makes a card, each friend secretly writes one square on yours, and the cards reveal on 1 January (or any date you pick). Tick squares through the year; a friend confirms the square they wrote. First line wins.
